A 594B model you can download: Inkling 2 Small goes Apache 2.0
Thinking Machines Lab (Mira Murati's company) released the weights a week after the closed launch, following outside safety testing. It is a mixture-of-experts model that uses only 17B parameters per token, accepts images, and runs on SGLang, vLLM and Unsloth.
