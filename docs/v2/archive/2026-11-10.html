k-means got 10x faster: Meta's e-means ships in Faiss 1.16
Meta FAIR open-sourced e-means, which replaces Lloyd's full passes with mini-batch updates. It reaches the same loss as 25 rounds of classic k-means in about 1.2 to 3 rounds, with no new knobs to tune. Faiss 1.16.0 packages it with GPU kernels.

WHY IT MATTERS TO YOU
We cluster all the time: customer and outlet segments, embeddings, vector index training. Hours of clustering could become minutes.
TRY IT IN 5 MINUTES
Upgrade Faiss to 1.16 in a scratch environment, run e-means (in faiss/contrib/e_means) next to your current k-means on one real dataset, and compare time and inertia.
