Set Copilot code review billing to Member in policy settings before 30 Dec
You can lock Copilot code review billing to Member in your Copilot policy settings to prevent automatic charges to your organization. Starting 30 Dec, GitHub will switch unset billing policies from Default (Member) to Organization for Copilot Business and Copilot Enterprise accounts.

WHY IT MATTERS TO YOU
Organization billing requires AI Credits paid usage and can increase your organization's spending. Keeping Member selected ensures code reviews by licensed members continue using each member's Copilot entitlement instead of billing the organization that owns the repository.
TRY IT IN 5 MINUTES
Open your enterprise or organization Copilot policy settings and check the Copilot code review billing option. If it shows Default (Member), select Member to keep your current billing after the default changes. If your enterprise manages this setting, ask an enterprise owner to select Member.
ACT TODAY
Select Member before 30 Dec if you want code reviews by licensed members to keep using their Copilot entitlements instead of switching to Organization billing.
WATCH OUT
- Accounts without AI Credits paid usage enabled, with a billing lock, or with no billable customer will be set to Member instead.
- If you already chose Member or Organization, your choice stays in effect, and the separate setting for members without a Copilot license does not change.
