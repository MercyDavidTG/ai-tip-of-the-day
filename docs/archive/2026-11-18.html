Gemini 4 Hydrogen lands: Carbon plans, Hydrogen does the work
Two days after Gemini 4 Carbon opened up, Google launched Hydrogen, its fast everyday model that replaces the Flash name. In Antigravity, the new Orchestrator mode lets Carbon plan while Hydrogen executes and checks the steps, so you keep Carbon quality on fewer tokens.

WHY IT MATTERS TO YOU
Big model plans, small model executes is now a product feature. It is the cheapest way to run agent pipelines, and the same split works with any vendor.
TRY IT IN 5 MINUTES
In AI Studio, run one of your usual prompts on Hydrogen and on your current model. Compare the answer, the speed and the token cost side by side.
WATCH OUT
Most numbers are Google's own, and the top scores need xhigh thinking, which costs about 70% more per task. On terminal work Claude Sonnet 5.5 still leads in independent tests. Try it on your own prompts before switching.
