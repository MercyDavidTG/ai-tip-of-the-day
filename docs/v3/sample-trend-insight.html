FRONTIER AI
Why AI that passes once can still fail on rerun
FRIDAY'S RIDDLE: I am a number, yet not a number, and I am not even equal to myself. What am I? Answer: NaN. NaN == NaN is False, so use isna() or math.isnan() to find missing values.
THE TREND
Google Deepmind's Humanity's First Exam tests AI reliability, not peak performance. Top models solve nearly every task family at least once, yet none stays error-free across repeated runs.
TRY THIS
Before trusting a model with a task, rerun it several times with small variations and check every answer.
1
Pick one everyday task you give an AI model.
2
Run it several times, rewording slightly each time.
3
Count error-free runs, not just one success.
THINK ABOUT IT
Are we judging AI tools by their best run, when our work depends on every run?
