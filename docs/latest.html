AlphaGenome 2 predicts what a DNA change does in each cell type
Google DeepMind's new model was trained on more than 100 million single-cell profiles across 68 tissues. On the TenK10K immune-cell dataset it found nearly twice as many causal variants as the best earlier tools (30% vs 16%). It is available for non-commercial research through the AlphaGenome API.

WHY IT MATTERS TO YOU
Steal the design idea: one model conditioned on context (cell type) instead of one model per context. Same pattern as one demand model conditioned on store, region or channel.
TRY IT IN 5 MINUTES
Read the DeepMind post and look at how the context is fed to the model. Note one place in our forecasting or pricing work where we keep separate models that could share one.
